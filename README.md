## 🏛️ About Janata Chitralaya
Serving both the government and private sectors across Odisha since 2000, Janata Chitralaya provides authentic costumes and accessories for:
- **School & College Annual Functions & Competitions** (DAV Public Schools, KIIT International, Sai International, BJEM, Loyola, Mothers Public School, Stewart, DPS Kalinga, Ravenshaw University, Utkal University, Rama Devi Women's University, BJB College, etc.)
- **Classical Odissi & Folk Dance Ensembles** (Sambalpuri, Odissi filigree jewelry, headpieces, alta)
- **Mythological, Historical & Jatra Plays** (Kings, Queens, Ramayana, Mahabharata, handcrafted Mukuts, gada, swords)
- **Pre-Wedding, OTT, Cinema & Portfolio Shoots**
- **Rabindra Mandap Stage Makeup & Behind-The-Scenes Artistry**
