Rabindra Mandap Stage Makeup & Artistry

From iconic cultural productions at Rabindra Mandap, Utkal Mandap, to stage competitions and dramas across Odisha, our backstage team brings characters to life behind the scenes. We use hypoallergenic, stage-certified pigments that withstand intense theatrical stage lights and performance hours.

  • Mythological Deities & Demons: Lord Shiva, Kali, Durga, Krishna, Ravana, Narasimha, and demon masks.
  • Classical Odissi & Dance Styling: Authentic alta designs, traditional eyes, tahiya headpiece fixing, and ornaments.
  • Historical Drama Prosthetics: Wounds, aging lines, royal beards, moustaches, and warrior face paint.
  • On-Site Makeup Artist Deployment: Available to visit schools, auditoriums, and shooting locations on request.
